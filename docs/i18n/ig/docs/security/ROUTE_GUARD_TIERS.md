## Nchịkọta

A na-ekewa ụzọ API njikwa OmniRoute niile n’otu n’ime ọkwa nchekwa atọ.
Nhazi a anaghị agbanwe agbanwe, akọwapụtara ya na `src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`,
a na-enyochakwa ya tupu ngalaba nyocha njirimara ọ bụla ọzọ amalite.

## Ọkwa

### Ọkwa 1 — LOCAL_ONLY

**Ndị na-amanye ya:** `isLocalOnlyPath(path)` → nyocha loopback host  
**Ụzọ mgbapụ:** Enweghị nke ndabara. E nwere naanị obere mwepu maka ụzọ ndị dị na
`LOCAL_ONLY_MANAGE_SCOPE_BYPASS_PREFIXES` mgbe arịrịọ ahụ nwere API key ziri ezi
nke nwere scope `manage` (lee [Mwepu scope manage](#manage-scope-carve-out)).

Ụzọ ndị a na-ebido child processes ma ọ bụ na-eme runtime code. Ime ka okporo ụzọ
na-abụghị loopback nwee ike iru ha ga-enye onye mwakpo nwetara JWT ziri ezi (dịka,
site na tunnel Cloudflared/Ngrok) ohere ịkpalite mmalite process — otu ụdị CVE
a maara nke ọma ([GHSA-fhh6-4qxv-rpqj](https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-fhh6-4qxv-rpqj)).

**Ihe GHSA-fhh6-4qxv-rpqj bụ (ụdị mwakpo ahụ):** sava management/agent
na-ekpughe endpoint nke na-ebido subprocess (`npm install`, `node`, browser,
proxy, `git`, `tar`, …). Ọ bụrụ na enwere ike iru endpoint ahụ site n'èzí host —
n'ihi na onye na-ahụ maka ya tinyere OmniRoute n'azụ tunnel nginx/Cloudflare/Tailscale
ma JWT agbapụ, ma ọ bụ na ahazighị auth nke ọma — onye mwakpo ahụ na-agbanwe
"ịkpọ API" ka ọ bụrụ "ịgba command na host" (remote code execution). OmniRoute
na-egbochi nke a site n'ịmanye **nnyocha loopback host n'enweghị ọnọdụ ọ bụla,
tupu nyocha auth ọ bụla**, n'ụzọ ọ bụla nwere ike ịmalite process: token gbapụtara
site na tunnel ka enweghị ike iru ebe a na-amalite process.

**Nchịkọta LOCAL_ONLY zuru ezu.** Ebe ntụkwasị obi kachasị bụ
`LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES` / `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PATTERNS` dị na
`src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`; tebụl dị n'okpuru na-egosipụta ọnọdụ dị ugbu a.
Gate `check-route-guard-membership` na-edepụta `route.ts` ọ bụla dị n'okpuru
prefixes ndị nwere ike ịmalite process ma mee ka CI daa ma ọ bụrụ na a naghị
ahazi nke ọ bụla dịka local-only.

#### Mwepu pụrụ iche nke manage scope

Enwere ike ịnweta otu obere akụkụ nke ụzọ LOCAL_ONLY site na adreesị na-abụghị loopback ma ọ bụrụ na
naanị ma ọ bụrụ na arịrịọ ahụ nwere `Authorization: Bearer <api-key>` nke
metadata ya gụnyere scope `manage` (ma ọ bụ `admin`). A na-achịkwa mwepu a
n'ụzọ doro anya maka ụzọ ọ bụla site na `LOCAL_ONLY_MANAGE_SCOPE_BYPASS_PREFIXES` ka
ndabara maka ụzọ LOCAL_ONLY ọhụrụ ọ bụla nọgide bụrụ strict-loopback. A ka na-ajụ
arịrịọ na-enweghị nkwenye njirimara na arịrịọ nwere key na-enweghị manage site na
`403 LOCAL_ONLY`.

Taa, naanị prefix a pụrụ ịgafe bụ `/api/mcp/`. E wepụrụ `/api/cli-tools/runtime/` na
`/api/services/` n'ebumnuche n'ihi na ha nwere ike ịmalite subprocess ọ bụla
(`npm install`, `node`), nke bụ kpọmkwem ụdị CVE e guzobere ọkwa
LOCAL_ONLY iji gbochie.

**#7895 — scope dị warara nke `mcp:connect`:** mwepu `/api/mcp/` NA-ANABATAKWA
Bearer key nwere scope dị warara `mcp:connect`
(`src/shared/constants/managementScopes.ts::MCP_CONNECT_SCOPE`), nke a na-enyocha site na
`hasMcpConnectOrManageScope()` n'ime `src/server/authz/policies/management.ts`.
Nke a metụtara `/api/mcp/` NAANỊ — `mcp:connect` anaghị enye ikike ọ bụla n'ụzọ
management ọzọ ọ bụla (gụnyere prefix mwepu LOCAL_ONLY ọ bụla ọzọ, ma ọ bụrụ na agbakwunye ya
n'ọdịnihu), ma e wepụrụ ya n'ebumnuche na
`MANAGEMENT_API_KEY_SCOPES`. Key nwere `manage`/`admin` ka na-agafe
mwepu ahụ dịka ọ dị na mbụ; `mcp:connect` bụ nhọrọ nwere ikike dị ala
maka ndị na-akpọ MCP naanị site n'ebe dị anya, ndị na-ekwesịghị ịchọ ohere management sara mbara.

| Na-abụghị loopback, enweghị Bearer                           | `/api/mcp/*`               | 403 LOCAL_ONLY               |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `manage`              | `/api/mcp/*`               | Kwe ka ọ gafee               |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `mcp:connect`         | `/api/mcp/*`               | Kwe ka ọ gafee               |

| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `mcp:connect`         | `/api/cli-tools/runtime/*` | 403 LOCAL_ONLY               |
| Na-abụghị loopback, Bearer nwere scope `manage`              | `/api/cli-tools/runtime/*` | 403 LOCAL_ONLY               |
| Loopback, Bearer ọ bụla/enweghị Bearer                       | LOCAL_ONLY ọ bụla          | Kwe ka ọ gafee (gate gafere) |

#### Nduzi onye na-arụ ọrụ & nyocha ndekọ

Ọ bụrụ na ị na-agba OmniRoute n'azụ reverse proxy ma ọ bụ tunnel (nginx, Caddy, Cloudflare
Tunnel, Tailscale, Ngrok), nyocha loopback ka na-echebe ụzọ ndị nwere ike
ịmalite usoro ndị dị n'elu — a na-ajụ arịrịọ nke adreesị client ya na-abụghị loopback site na
`403 LOCAL_ONLY` **tupu auth amalite**, ya mere JWT e kpughere enweghị ike iru mmalite usoro. Ọrụ abụọ
nke onye na-arụ ọrụ ka fọdụrụ:

- **Anwala “idozi” 403 site n’ịgha adreesị IP onye ahịa ka ọ bụrụ loopback.** Ịtọ
  `X-Forwarded-For: 127.0.0.1`, ma ọ bụ iji proxy nke na-edegharị adreesị isi mmalite ka ọ
  bụrụ loopback, na-emepeghachi kpọmkwem ụdị RCE nke ọkwa a na-emechi. Mee ka
  dashboard/API dị site na proxy — emela ka ụzọ ndị nwere ike ịmalite usoro dị.
- **Debe ngafe manage-scope ka ọ pere mpe.** Naanị `/api/mcp/` ka enwere ike ịgafe, ma
  naanị site na igodo API nwere scope `manage`. A pụghị itinye
  `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PREFIXES` na ndepụta ngafe ma ọlị — schema zod na-ajụ ha, ebe
  `isLocalOnlyBypassableByManageScope` na-egbochi ha n’oge arụmọrụ (nchebe nwere ọtụtụ ọkwa),
  nke a bụ ihe dashboard na-ekwu mgbe ọ sịrị “enweghị ike ime ka a gafee ya”. Ihe na-ebunye
  snapshot n’oge arụmọrụ na-ewepụkwa ndenye ndị dị otu ahụ na ahịrị ntọala echekwara, ebe
  mbubata JSON ochie (`/api/settings/import-json`) anaghị ede igodo ngafe ndị ahụ ma ọlị.
  Ndenye `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES` ọ bụla ga-adị ma na deny-list ma ọ bụ
  a ga-enyocha ya dịka nke ruru eru maka ngafe (`tests/unit/authz/local-only-bypass-spawn-denylist.test.ts`). Ụzọ
  nwere dynamic-segment na static-path ndị nwere ike ịmalite usoro n’okpuru `/api/providers/` (dịka `/login`,
  `/refresh-cursor`) ka ndị enyemaka `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PATTERNS` /
  `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PATTERN_ANCESTORS` dabere na regex, ndị dị na
  `src/shared/constants/spawnCapablePrefixes.ts`, na-ekpuchi, ọ bụghị array
  `SPAWN_CAPABLE_PREFIXES` dị larịị — array dị larịị ahụ ga-ekpuchirịrị
  prefix `/api/providers/` niile iji jide ha, nke ga-eme ka osisi ụzọ
  ndị dashboard dịpụrụ adịpụ ji eme CRUD nke provider gbasaa oke karịa mkpa.

**Inyocha ohere nnweta** — iji nyochaa na ọ dịghị ihe si n’èzí host na-erute ụzọ ndị a:

- Mepee **Authorization Inventory** na `/dashboard/settings/security`: ọ na-egosi
  ndepụta prefix LOCAL_ONLY dị ugbu a, prefix ndị enwere ike ịgafe, na setị
  ndị nwere ike ịmalite usoro nke a kpebiri n’oge compile (“enweghị ike ime ka a gafee ya”).
- Jiri grep chọọ prefix ndị dị n’elu n’ime ndekọ reverse-proxy / access gị, tinyere
  adreesị onye ahịa na-abụghị loopback. Ọ bụla n’ime ha nke zaghachiri `200` kama
  `403 LOCAL_ONLY` pụtara na proxy na-ezo ezigbo IP onye ahịa — dozie proxy ahụ.
- `403 LOCAL_ONLY` dị na ndekọ OmniRoute maka otu n’ime ụzọ ndị a bụ guard
  na-arụ ọrụ dịka e zubere, ọ bụghị njehie a ga-egbochi.

### Ọkwa 2 — ALWAYS_PROTECTED

**Onye na-amanye ya:** `isAlwaysProtectedPath(path)` → na-amafere ngafe `requireLogin=false`
**Ngafe:** Ọ dịghị mgbe `requireLogin=false`; JWT dị mkpa mgbe niile

Ụzọ ndị a na-emebi ihe ma ọ bụ enweghị ike ịlaghachi azụ. Ikwe ka ha rụọ ọrụ na nrụnye
“enweghị okwuntughe” ga-apụta na onye ọ bụla nọ n’otu LAN ahụ nwere ike ihichapụ database ma ọ bụ kwụsị
usoro server ahụ.

| Ụzọ                                       | Ihe kpatara ya                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/api/shutdown`                           | Na-akwụsị usoro server ahụ                                                   |
| `/api/settings/database`                  | Mbupụ, mbubata, na ihichapụ database                                         |
| `/api/db-backups`                         | Ohere nnweta archive ndabere database zuru ezu                               |
| `/api/settings/export-json`               | Na-ebupụ blob ntọala niile (gụnyere ihe nzuzo)                               |
| `/api/settings/import-json`               | Na-anọchi blob ntọala niile                                                  |
| `/api/providers/health-autopilot/actions` | Na-eme omume ndozi autopilot                                                 |
| `/api/settings/obsidian`                  | Na-emepụta kredenshial WebDAV enwere ike iji ọzọ maka mgbọrọgwụ vault ọ bụla |

**Nzaghachi mgbe emebiri iwu:** `401 Authentication required`

`/api/settings/obsidian` na-ekpuchi child `/webdav` ya: `POST` na-atụ aka ọrụ faịlụ WebDAV —
nke custom Node layer na-enye tupu Next.js, n’èzí pipeline a — n’ebe mgbọrọgwụ onye kpọrọ ya họọrọ
ma na-eweghachi kredenshial Basic e mepụtara ọhụrụ, `DELETE` na-agbanwe ha, ebe parent `POST` na-echekwa
token REST API nke Obsidian. GHSA-62vw zoro naanị mkpughe okwuntughe nke `GET`; inye kredenshial ahụ
ka dị n’ọkwa fail-open (GHSA-7pq4-8pvv-rx7r). `enableObsidianVaultSync()` na-ajụkwa
vault nke bụ data directory, dị n’ime ya, ma ọ bụ nke nwere ya n’ime onwe ya.

### Bootstrap nrụnye ọhụrụ bụ naanị maka loopback — dabere na ezigbo peer, ọ bụghị `Host`

Mgbe a hazibeghị okwuntughe njikwa ọ bụla (ma `INITIAL_PASSWORD` adịghị), `isAuthRequired()` dị na
`src/shared/utils/apiAuth.ts` na-ahapụ bootstrap na-amaghị onye ọrụ ka o ghe oghe **naanị maka loopback peers**.
A na-ekpebi loopback site na trusted peer signals ndị a, n’usoro: ezigbo TCP peer nke e tinyere stamp token
(`PEER_IP_HEADER` + `VIA_PROXY_HEADER`, nke policy na-ahụ), mkpebi
`AUTHZ_HEADER_PEER_LOCALITY` nke pipeline n’onwe ya (nke route handlers na-ahụ, nke a na-atụkwasị obi naanị mgbe
edobere `OMNIROUTE_PEER_STAMP_TOKEN`), ma ọ bụ ezigbo socket peer maka ndị na-akpọ ozugbo. A naghị elele `Host` /
`nextUrl.hostname` ma ọlị, tinyere na ide okwuntughe mbụ
(`POST /api/settings/require-login`) nọ n’okpuru otu mmachi ahụ kama imeghe ya nye
network peer niile (GHSA-7pq4-8pvv-rx7r). `managementPolicy` na-ebufe mkpebi `peerContext`
nke ya kpọmkwem, ya mere header arịrịọ ORIGINAL (tupu strip) anaghị ekpebi ya.

### Ọkwa 3 — MANAGEMENT (ndabara)

Ụzọ njikwa ndị ọzọ niile. Achọrọ auth belụsọ ma ahaziri `requireLogin=false`.
Token CLI nwere ike ime authentication maka ụzọ ndị a (loopback + HMAC ziri ezi).

## Usoro nnyocha

```
managementPolicy.evaluate(ctx)
  1. isLocalOnlyPath(path)?
     → loopback                                  → gaa n'ihu
     → non-loopback, Bearer nwere manage-scope
        NA isLocalOnlyBypassableByManageScope   → kwe (management_key)
     → ma ọ bụghị ya                             → jụ 403 LOCAL_ONLY
  2. isInternalModelSyncRequest(ctx)?
     → kwe (system)
  3. hasValidCliToken(headers)?
     → kwe (cli) [loopback + nyocha HMAC nke timingSafeEqual]
  4. isAlwaysProtectedPath(path) ma ọ bụ requireLogin=true?
     → isDashboardSessionAuthenticated?
        → kwe (dashboard_session)
     → Bearer nwere manage-scope n'ụzọ a na-apụghị ịgafe?
        → kwe (management_key)
     → jụ 401/403
  5. requireLogin=false?
     → kwe (anonymous)
```

Alaka manage-scope nke Nzọụkwụ 1 bụ naanị ụzọ e ji njirimara akwadoro nke nwere ike imezu ụzọ
LOCAL_ONLY; ọnọdụ ọdịda auth-backend na-eweghachi 503 (ọ bụghị 403), ka DB kubie ume ghara iji nwayọọ gbanwee gaa na "jụ".

## Ịgbakwunye ụzọ ọhụrụ nwere ike ime spawn

1. Tinye prefix nke ụzọ ahụ na `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES` dị na
   `src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`
2. Tinye ule na `tests/unit/authz/routeGuard.test.ts` nke na-egosi na
   `isLocalOnlyPath()` na-eweghachi true maka prefix ọhụrụ ahụ
3. **Agafela nzọụkwụ a ma ọlị** — lee Iwu Siri Ike #15 na `CLAUDE.md`
4. Kpebie: ụzọ a ò kwesịkwara ịdị na `LOCAL_ONLY_MANAGE_SCOPE_BYPASS_PREFIXES`?
   Azịza ndabara bụ **mba**. Họrọ ya naanị mgbe ụzọ ahụ dị nchebe ikpughere onye
   ji manage-scope (ya bụ, ọ naghị eme spawn nke koodu ọ bụla onye ọrụ na-achịkwa).

## Ịgbakwunye ụzọ manage-scope nwere ike ịgafe

1. Kwenye na ụzọ ahụ anaghị agba koodu ma ọ bụ iwu onye ọrụ nyere. Ọ bụrụ na ọ
   na-eme ya, kwụsị — mwepu a abụghị ngwa kwesịrị ekwesị.
2. Tinye prefix ahụ na njedebe nke `LOCAL_ONLY_MANAGE_SCOPE_BYPASS_PREFIXES` dị na
   `src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`
3. Tinye mkpuchi ule na `tests/unit/authz/management-policy.test.ts` maka ụdị
   arịrịọ anọ niile: enweghị Bearer (403), manage Bearer (kwe), Bearer na-abụghị manage
   (403), yana ule nlọghachi-mperi nke prefix ọ bụla nke na-ahụ na `/api/cli-tools/runtime/*` ka
   bụ naanị strict-loopback ọbụna mgbe e nwere manage Bearer.

## Idekọ Tier Nchekwa na OpenAPI

Mgbe ị na-agbakwunye ụzọ ọhụrụ na `docs/openapi.yaml`, tinye vendor extension
dabara adaba ma ọ bụrụ na `routeGuard.ts` ekewala ụzọ ahụ n'ụdị:

| Nkewa routeGuard.ts          | Nkọwa YAML                 | Mmanye                                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES`    | `x-loopback-only: true`    | A na-egbochi non-loopback n'enweghị ihe ọzọ     |
| `ALWAYS_PROTECTED_API_PATHS` | `x-always-protected: true` | A chọrọ auth ọbụna mgbe `requireLogin=false`    |
| Ụzọ admin/debug dị n'ime     | `x-internal: true`         | Ezoro ya na /dashboard/api-endpoints na ndabara |
| Enweghị (auth ọha / ọkọlọtọ) | (ọ dịghị nkọwa achọrọ)     | Nnweta ọkọlọtọ nke `requireLogin` na-achịkwa    |

### Nkwado izi ezi

Skrip abụọ na-amanye nkwekọrịta n'etiti nkọwa YAML na `routeGuard.ts`:

- `scripts/check/check-openapi-coverage.mjs` — na-ada ma ọ bụrụ na mkpuchi < 99%
- `scripts/check/check-openapi-security-tiers.mjs` — na-ada ma ọ bụrụ na nkọwa `x-loopback-only` ma ọ bụ
  `x-always-protected` dị iche na konstants oge compile

Skrip abụọ ahụ na-agba na hook pre-commit nakwa na CI.

### Iwu False Positive

Ọ bụrụ na e tinyere nkọwa `x-always-protected` ma ọ bụ `x-loopback-only` na ụzọ nke NA-ADỊGHỊ na
konstant `routeGuard.ts`, skrip mkpuchi ahụ ga-ada. Ndozi ya bụ mgbe niile ime ka
YAML kwekọọ n'ihe `routeGuard.ts` na-amanye n'ezie — ọ bụghị ịgbakwunye ụzọ na `routeGuard.ts`
n'etinyeghịkwa lojiik mmanye ahụ.
